About StoryCast

Every voice deserves to be heard.

Stories connect us, preserve our culture, and help us understand experiences beyond our own.

Our Story

StoryCast is a storytelling platform created to bring meaningful stories to people through video, audio, and transcripts. We believe that every person’s experience has something valuable to teach, whether it is a personal journey, a cultural tradition, a life lesson, or an experience from the community.

Our goal is to make these stories easy to discover, watch, listen to, and understand. By bringing different voices and perspectives together, StoryCast creates space for people to learn from one another, appreciate different experiences, and build stronger connections.

Our Mission

To make meaningful stories accessible to everyone by providing a simple and engaging platform for discovering and experiencing stories through different formats.

What We Believe

  • Every voice matters

    Everyone has experiences worth sharing, and every story deserves the opportunity to be heard.

  • Stories help us learn

    Personal experiences, culture, and shared knowledge can inspire new ideas and help us see the world differently.

  • Stories should be accessible

    Video, audio, and transcripts give people different ways to experience and understand each story.

Our Vision

We envision a world where meaningful stories can cross cultural and personal boundaries, bringing people closer through shared experiences, knowledge, and understanding.

Our accessibility commitment

We design and test StoryCast against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. StoryCast is a student prototype, so we describe what has been tested and what is still missing rather than claiming full compliance.

  • Captions

    Videos support closed captions through the HTML <track> element. Three of the five videos have English captions; each story page says whether captions are available.

  • Transcripts

    We only publish transcripts that have been checked against the recording. Each story page shows whether one is available.

  • Keyboard navigation

    Every link, button and media control can be reached with the Tab key, starting with a “Skip to main content” link.

  • Visible focus

    The item you are on is marked with a thick, high-contrast outline.

  • Readable typography

    Body text is at least 16px with comfortable line spacing and line lengths, and it resizes with your browser settings.

  • Color contrast

    Text and controls are checked against the WCAG contrast ratios (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and controls). Links are underlined, so they never rely on color alone.

  • Responsive layouts

    Pages reflow from wide desktop screens down to 320px-wide phones without sideways scrolling.

  • Reduced motion

    If your device is set to reduce motion, StoryCast turns off smooth scrolling and transitions.

Known limitations

  • Two of the five videos (the music video and the BBC story) do not yet have closed captions or a transcript.
  • Testing has used automated checks and keyboard testing in Chrome. Screen reader testing is still to be done.

Get involved

Share your story

We are looking for honest, personal stories told in your own words: a turning point, a tradition, a lesson or an experience from your community. Stories of 2 to 10 minutes work best, and anyone you name should agree to be included.

Accessibility feedback

If something on StoryCast is hard to use, tell us which page you were on, the device and any assistive technology you use, and what happened. This prototype does not collect messages yet; contact details will be added when the project is published.
